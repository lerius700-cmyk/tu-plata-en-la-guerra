Gaza Genocide: a Collective Crime
Report of the Special Rapporteur on the situation of human rights in the
Palestinian territories occupied since 1967, Francesca Albanese
20 October 2025 · 24 pages

The ongoing genocide in Gaza is a collective crime, sustained by the complicity of influential Third States that have enabled longstanding systemic violations of international law by Israel. Framed by colonial narratives that dehumanize the Palestinians, this live-streamed atrocity has been facilitated through Third States' direct support, material aid, diplomatic protection and, in some cases, active participation.

Many States, primarily Western ones, have facilitated, legitimized and eventually normalized the genocidal campaign perpetrated by Israel.

Sectors of complicity

DiplomáticoCobertura en consejos de seguridad, vetos, abstenciones
MilitarComercio de armas, intercambio de inteligencia, entrenamiento conjunto
EconómicoComercio, inversiones, transferencia de tecnología
"Humanitario"Instrumentalización de la ayuda

Focusing on the aid and assistance that Third States have provided to the illegal Israeli occupation and its genocide of the Palestinian people, the report identifies four sectors of support: diplomatic, military, economic and "humanitarian".

63 Estados

mencionados en el informe como cómplices del genocidio. 18 de ellos presentaron respuesta formal antes de la publicación. El texto original lista: EE.UU., Alemania, Reino Unido, Italia, Francia, Países Bajos, Polonia, Austria, Irlanda, Dinamarca, Grecia, España, Rumania y Egipto (entre otros).

Cifra clave: "EE.UU. y Alemania solos han proporcionado el 90% de las importaciones de armas a Israel".
EE.UU.: 742 transferencias de armas desde oct/2023; 7 vetos en Consejo de Seguridad; paquete armamentístico de USD 26,4 mil millones.
Reino Unido: 600+ misiones de vigilancia sobre Gaza desde oct/2023.

All 63 States mentioned in the report were provided the opportunity to comment on factual errors or inaccuracies; 18 States submitted a reply.
